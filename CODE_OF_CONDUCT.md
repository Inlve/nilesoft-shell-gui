# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Shell Studio a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, trolling, personal
attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Report incidents privately through the repository's security advisory
channel. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
